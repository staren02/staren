05 — HOW I WORK

Start simple.
Keep going.

No big formula.
Just a way of working that makes sense to me.

01Find the idea

Figure out what the thing is supposed to do before getting lost in how it should look.

02Build the first version

Get something working, even when it is rough. A working problem is easier to understand.

03Notice what is wrong

Then fix the parts that actually matter instead of polishing everything at once.

04Learn what I needed

The gaps usually become the next thing I study. That is how the work keeps moving.
